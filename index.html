faq.txt · Notepad_□×
What is SCULPT?

An AI-powered token creation launchpad on Solana. You describe an idea and the AI carves it into a fully branded project: name, ticker, logo, banner, description, website and metadata.

What are the four stages?

Raw, Carving, Refining and Finished. Your statue moves through them as the project is generated and refined, and the finished statue becomes the face of your token.

Can I change what it makes?

Yes. Ask in plain words: make the logo more minimal, change the colors, give the website a different style. Each request is another strike of the chisel.

Who holds my keys?

You do. SCULPT prepares the transaction; your wallet signs it. It never takes custody of your keys or tokens.

How do fees work?

Under the proposed model, 50% of protocol fees buy $SCULPT, 30% fund workshop development and 20% go to sculptor rewards.

When does it launch?

Launch details, the $SCULPT contract address and on-chain deploys are announced on X.
